WHY SHELFSENSE?

Every product counts.

For a small retailer, every product represents an investment. Unnoticed expiry dates, missing stock and poor inventory records can lead to avoidable losses. ShelfSense brings essential inventory management tools into one accessible platform, helping retailers make informed decisions and manage their shops with greater confidence.

WHAT WE OFFER

Everything in one place.

Practical tools designed to make everyday retail management easier.

01 / INVENTORY

Stock Management

Maintain organised product records and keep track of the quantities available in your shop.

02 / EXPIRY

Expiry Monitoring

Identify products approaching their expiry dates and take action before they become a loss.

03 / SCANNING

Barcode Scanning

Register products and update recorded stock quantities using camera-based barcode scanning.

04 / ALERTS

Low Stock Alerts

Know when products reach their minimum stock levels and identify what needs restocking.

05 / LOSS CONTROL

Inventory Accountability

Compare recorded quantities with physical stock to help identify discrepancies and investigate potential losses.

06 / SALES

Sales Records

Record transactions and review sales history to understand your shop's activity.
