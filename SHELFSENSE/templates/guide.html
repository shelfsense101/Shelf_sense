ShelfSense User Guide

Everything you need to manage your shop inventory.

1. Create an account

Register using your name, shop name, phone number or email, and password.

2. Add products

Enter the product name, brand, category, quantity, purchase date and expiry date.

3. Scan barcodes

Use Quick Scan to identify products and update stock when an item is scanned.

4. Monitor expiry dates

Check the expiry section to identify products that are approaching their expiry dates or have expired.

5. Manage low stock

Set a minimum stock threshold to receive alerts when quantities become low.

6. Record sales

Record product sales and view your sales history and revenue.
